What it is

Perfumaria Studio is a personal tool used by a single creator to publish their own perfume review videos to their own TikTok account (@mateusaao_legend).

The videos are recorded and edited by the creator on their own computer. Perfumaria Studio then uses the official TikTok Content Posting API to send the finished video, with its caption, to that same TikTok account, either as a draft to be completed in the TikTok app or as a post.

What it does not do

It is not a public service: no one else can sign up or connect an account. It does not read, collect or store data from other TikTok users, and it does not like, comment, follow or interact with other accounts.
