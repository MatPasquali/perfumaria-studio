Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Perfumaria Studio ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @mateusaao_legend ("the operator") to publish the operator's own videos to that account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data the app handles

  • TikTok authorization data: the access token, refresh token and open ID that TikTok issues when the operator authorizes the app, with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  • Basic profile and creator information of the operator's own account (display name, avatar, available privacy options and maximum video length), used only to prepare a post correctly.
  • The operator's own videos and captions, which the operator creates and chooses to upload.

The app does not collect, read or store any data about other TikTok users.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload and publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

3. Where the data is stored

Tokens and files are stored only on the operator's personal computer. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are deleted when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app, in the settings for apps connected to the account, which immediately invalidates the app's tokens.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult operator.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
